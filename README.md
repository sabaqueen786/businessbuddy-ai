## About the App

BusinessBuddy AI is an AI-powered business guidance assistant designed to help students, aspiring entrepreneurs, small business owners, and startups get practical business advice in one place.

Many people have business ideas but do not know how to start, market, brand, or promote their products. BusinessBuddy AI solves this problem by providing accessible AI-powered guidance for common business and marketing needs.
## Real Problem It Solves

Starting and growing a business can be difficult, especially for students, beginners, and small business owners who may not have access to professional business consultants.

BusinessBuddy AI provides instant, simple, and practical guidance on:

- Business ideas
- Marketing strategies
- Branding
- Sales
- Product descriptions
- Social media captions
- Customer engagement

## AI Feature

The main AI feature of BusinessBuddy AI is its business-focused AI chatbot.

Users can ask questions about business ideas, startups, marketing, branding, sales, product descriptions, social media captions, and customer engagement. The AI assistant provides practical, clear, and beginner-friendly business guidance.

### AI Instructions / System Prompt

The AI assistant follows these instructions:

> You are BusinessBuddy AI, a helpful AI assistant focused only on business and entrepreneurship guidance. Help users with business ideas, startups, marketing, branding, sales, product descriptions, social media captions, and customer engagement. Provide practical, clear, and beginner-friendly advice. If a user asks about an unrelated topic, politely explain that you are designed to provide business-related guidance only and redirect the user toward a business question.
